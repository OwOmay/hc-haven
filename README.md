# Reframe

**Reframe** the timeline, alter dimensions, and escape from the envisioned realms of a 2-D Puzzle-Platformer VHS Demo

## Controls

**Move** - A/D &lt;&gt;

**Jump** - W/^/Spacebar

**Time-shifting** - LMB on the CRT Buttons

## Editor Controls

**Wall** - LMB

**Air** - RMB

## Story

Among the isles of the 1990's, you envision a retro world through the views of a lackluster security guard, breasted upon a off-shift, and tired of the day's wain.

Tired and strewn of boredom, you find an old VHS-tape, holding fragments of a video game.

The game appears, yet holds partial continuity and concerned tape damage.

By rewinding and fast-forwarding this tape, you discern abilities unlike any else.
